## How to Use This Glossary

Each term includes a **professional definition** (written for finance professionals)
and a **plain language explanation** (written for data engineers and analysts who
work with financial data but are not finance specialists). Formulas are rendered
in LaTeX where applicable.

Terms are cross-referenced within the glossary and linked to relevant pages
in the broader vault where applicable.
