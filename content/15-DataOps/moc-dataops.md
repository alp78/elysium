# MOC: DataOps

DataOps applies Agile, DevOps, and Lean principles to data engineering -- covering everything from CI/CD for pipelines and shift-left testing to team organization and stakeholder management. This MOC groups the five pages by focus area: foundational practices first, then domain-specific operations, then team and leadership concerns.

## DataOps Principles and Platform Design — Practices, Validation, and Self-Service

Core DataOps methodology, index-specific operational patterns, and the self-service platform layer that enables non-engineers to consume data independently.

* [[dataops-principles-and-practices]] — the DataOps manifesto, CI/CD for data pipelines, shift-left testing, statistical process control, data contracts, DORA metrics, and the maturity model

* [[dataops-for-indices]] — parallel backtesting architecture, shadow calculations, blue-green data deployment, methodology versioning, regression testing, and incident response for index calculation platforms

* [[self-service-data-platform]] — data catalogs, semantic layers, governed self-service access, data products, data contracts, quality layers, and cost governance for enabling analysts without filing tickets

## Team Organization and Leadership — Roles, Collaboration, and Decision-Making

How to structure data teams and operate effectively as a senior engineer within complex organizations.

* [[data-team-organization]] — team topologies (centralized, embedded, hub-and-spoke, federated), role definitions, career ladders, RACI matrices, hiring strategies, and on-call rotation models

* [[leadership-and-collaboration]] — the code review as a teaching tool, technical design documents, Architecture Decision Records, stakeholder management, mentoring progression, managing technical debt, and blameless post-mortems with war room procedures
